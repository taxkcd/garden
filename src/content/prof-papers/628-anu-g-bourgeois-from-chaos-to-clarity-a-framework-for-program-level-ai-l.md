## Paper overview

This paper proposes a structured framework called Program-Level AI Learning Outcomes (PLAI-LOs) to help higher education programs clearly define what students should know and be able to do with generative AI (GenAI) by graduation. It addresses the current confusion and fragmentation in AI education by aligning institutional priorities, program goals, course objectives, and assignment policies to prepare students for a GenAI-augmented workforce.

### Why it matters

**Research problem:** Higher education lacks a clear, coherent structure for defining and implementing AI readiness across disciplines, programs, courses, and assignments, leading to mixed messages for students and fragmented expectations.

**Why it matters:** Industry increasingly demands AI proficiency, especially with generative AI, but students and faculty face uncertainty about how and when to use GenAI in learning. Without clear guidance, programs struggle to prepare graduates effectively for evolving workforce needs.

**Key contributions:**

- Introduction of the PLAI-LOs framework to define program-level AI learning outcomes that complement existing program learning outcomes.
- A backward design process for creating and iteratively refining PLAI-LOs aligned with institutional priorities and evolving GenAI technologies.
- Mapping PLAI-LOs to course-level outcomes and assignment-level GenAI policies to clarify acceptable AI use and learning goals.
- Examples of PLAI-LOs for undergraduate computer science and music programs illustrating discipline-specific AI readiness.
- A mechanism for monitoring and reporting PLAI-LO implementation progress to support curriculum redesign and program evaluation.
