## Paper overview

This paper presents a new evaluation framework and a multimodal dataset to assess how AI guidance affects human performance in physical tasks. The authors developed an augmented reality (AR) AI agent that provides interactive guidance for real-world tasks ranging from cooking to battlefield medicine. Human studies show that AI-assisted collaboration improves task success, reduces errors, and enhances learning outcomes.

### Why it matters

**Research problem:** Evaluating the effectiveness of AI agents in real-world human-in-the-loop physical task completion remains challenging due to the complexity of interactions and lack of structured evaluation frameworks that capture interactive guidance, execution quality, user experience, and learning outcomes.

**Why it matters:** AI agents have potential to enhance human performance in both everyday and high-stakes professional tasks, but without rigorous evaluation frameworks and real-world testing, it is difficult to measure their true impact and improve their design for practical deployment.

**Key contributions:**

- A structured evaluation framework and multimodal dataset for measuring AI-guided physical task performance in real-world human-AI collaboration.
- Development of an interactive AR-based AI agent that provides context-aware, real-time guidance for physical tasks.
- Empirical findings from human studies demonstrating improved task success, error reduction, and skill acquisition with AI assistance.
- Analysis of user interaction quality metrics such as clarity, proactivity, ease of use, satisfaction, and relevance.
- Cost-performance evaluation showing efficient AI inference costs relative to task success.
