## Paper overview

This paper discusses the opportunities and challenges of generative AI (GenAI), especially large language models (LLMs), in education. It gathers insights from nine experts on how GenAI can enhance teaching and learning through personalized content, feedback, and immersive environments, while also addressing ethical concerns, misuse, and the need for human-centered design and policy. The paper highlights the need for further research to understand GenAI's impact on education and to develop effective, ethical, and pedagogically sound applications.

### Why it matters

**Research problem:** How can generative AI technologies be effectively and ethically integrated into learning technologies and education, considering their opportunities, challenges, and implications?

**Why it matters:** GenAI tools like LLMs are rapidly transforming educational practices, offering potential improvements in learning design, feedback, and assessment. However, their limitations, ethical issues, and potential misuse pose risks that require careful study to ensure educational integrity and effectiveness.

**Key contributions:**

- Comprehensive expert synthesis on GenAI's educational opportunities and challenges.
- Identification of key areas impacted by GenAI: learning design, self-regulated learning, feedback generation, and domain-specific education.
- Discussion of ethical, pedagogical, and policy implications of GenAI adoption.
- Presentation of future research directions emphasizing human-centric design and evidence-based integration.

**Grounded in:** GenAI can enhance personalized learning pathways and real-time feedback.

**Tech stack:** TypeScript, React, Node.js, Express.js, OpenAI or Anthropic Claude API

**Data:** Simulated short student answers or code snippets entered by users; no external dataset required.

**Build it:**

1. Set up a React frontend with a form to input student answers or code snippets.
2. Implement a backend API in Node.js/Express that sends the input to an LLM API to generate feedback.
3. Display the LLM-generated personalized feedback in the frontend.
4. Add simple logic to tailor feedback style based on user-selected learning goals or difficulty levels.
5. Test the app with sample inputs to verify personalized feedback generation.

**Ships as:** A GitHub repo with a README demonstrating a working web app that generates personalized AI feedback, illustrating the concept of AI-enhanced personalized learning.

**Stretch goal:** Add a feature to track user progress and adapt feedback over multiple sessions to simulate a learning pathway.
