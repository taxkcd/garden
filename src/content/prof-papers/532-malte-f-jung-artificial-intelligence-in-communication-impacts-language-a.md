## Paper overview

This study investigates how AI-generated smart replies affect human communication. Using experiments with a custom messaging app, the authors found that AI can speed up conversations and increase positive emotional language, improving perceptions of cooperation and affiliation between people. However, if people think their partner is using AI-generated replies, they may judge them negatively. The study highlights both benefits and social challenges of AI in everyday communication.

### Why it matters

**Research problem:** The social consequences of using AI-generated messages, specifically algorithmic response suggestions (smart replies), in daily communication remain largely unexplored despite their widespread use.

**Why it matters:** AI-generated communication tools are used billions of times daily and can fundamentally change how people interact, influencing social relationships, trust, and cooperation. Understanding these effects is crucial for designing AI systems that support positive social outcomes and avoid unintended negative consequences.

**Key contributions:**

- Demonstrated that AI-generated smart replies increase communication speed and positive emotional language.
- Showed that actual use of AI replies improves perceptions of cooperation and affiliation, while perceived use leads to negative judgments.
- Developed a novel experimental platform (Moshi) for studying AI-mediated communication in real-time.
- Provided evidence that AI influences emotional content in conversations depending on the sentiment of the smart replies.
- Highlighted the discrepancy between negative social perceptions of AI use and the positive interpersonal effects of actual AI use.
