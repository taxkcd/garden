## Paper overview

This paper presents Exai-1, a novel multimodal transformer-based generative model that integrates RNA sequence embeddings with cell-free RNA abundance data from blood samples to improve disease detection, especially cancer, using liquid biopsies. By training on a large dataset of over 13,000 plasma and serum samples, Exai-1 can accurately reconstruct and denoise RNA profiles, generate synthetic data to augment training, and generalize well across different biofluids. This approach advances non-invasive diagnostics by leveraging artificial intelligence to capture complex biological signals from sparse and noisy RNA data.

### Why it matters

**Research problem:** Detecting diseases such as cancer early and non-invasively using cell-free RNA (cfRNA) in blood is challenging due to data sparsity, complexity, and limited sample availability, especially for rare cancers. Existing models struggle to generalize across different biofluids and to capture the complex interactions of circulating RNAs.

**Why it matters:** Liquid biopsy using cfRNA offers a less invasive, scalable method for early disease detection, monitoring minimal residual disease, and cancer subtype stratification. Improving computational methods to analyze cfRNA can enhance diagnostic accuracy and clinical utility, particularly where sample sizes are limited and data are noisy.

**Key contributions:**

- Development of Exai-1, a multimodal transformer-based generative model integrating RNA sequence and abundance data for cfRNA analysis.
- Training on the largest cfRNA dataset to date (over 13,000 plasma and serum samples) with over 306 billion tokens.
- Demonstration that Exai-1 accurately reconstructs masked cfRNA features with high fidelity (R2 ~0.89) and preserves biologically meaningful co-expression patterns.
- Exai-1 effectively denoises cfRNA data, outperforming existing methods like MAGIC at higher noise levels.
- Synthetic cfRNA profiles generated by Exai-1 improve cancer detection performance, especially in limited sample size scenarios.
