# 106 · Aspirational Affordances of AI

## Paper overview

This paper introduces the concept of aspirational affordances to explain how AI-generated cultural resources like images and narratives shape what individuals and groups imagine as possible futures for themselves. It argues that AI's influence on aspirations is powerful, often hidden, and concentrated among a few corporate systems, which can lead to 'aspirational harms'—ways AI limits or distorts the possibilities people can envision for their lives and societies. The paper distinguishes aspirational harms from other AI harms and illustrates them with case studies, emphasizing the need for careful evaluation and design to avoid reinforcing social injustices.

### Why it matters

**Research problem:** How AI-generated representations and narratives influence individuals' and groups' practical imagination and aspirations, potentially constraining or distorting the interpretive resources that shape what futures are imaginable and desirable.

**Why it matters:** AI systems increasingly mediate cultural and epistemic production, shaping not only perceptions but also aspirations. This influence is more potent, less publicly scrutinized, and concentrated in a few corporate-controlled systems, risking reinforcement of social injustices, narrowing of imagination, and entrenchment of harmful norms that affect agency and autonomy.

**Key contributions:**

- Introduction of the concept of aspirational affordances to describe how shared interpretive resources shape practical imagination and aspirations.
- Identification of three reasons why AI's influence on aspirational affordances requires special scrutiny: its potency and privacy, ecological impact, and oligopolistic concentration.
- Introduction of the novel concept of aspirational harm, distinct from representational and allocative harms, capturing how AI can distort or diminish interpretive resources that enable imagining alternative futures.
- Illustration of aspirational harms through case studies demonstrating how AI can narrow professional aspirations, obscure social-political transformations in AI-generated futures, and reinforce dominant narratives.
- Argument for the need to incorporate aspirational affordances into sociotechnical evaluations of AI systems.
