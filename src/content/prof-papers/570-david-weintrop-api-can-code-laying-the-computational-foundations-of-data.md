## Paper overview

This paper presents API Can Code, a high school curriculum designed to teach students the computational foundations of data science through engaging, interest-driven activities. Students use block-based programming to retrieve and analyze real-world data from public APIs, culminating in projects that connect data science to their personal interests. The curriculum aims to build both data science and computer science skills while fostering positive attitudes and confidence in students.

### Why it matters

**Research problem:** There is a growing need for K-12 curricula that introduce data science from a computational perspective, enabling students to work with real-world, messy datasets and engage meaningfully with data relevant to their lives. Existing curricula often focus on surface-level data engagement and lack computational depth.

**Why it matters:** Data science skills are increasingly important for navigating a data-driven world. Equipping students with computational foundations and authentic data experiences can broaden participation in computing and prepare them for future academic and career opportunities.

**Key contributions:**

- A classroom-tested, interest-driven high school data science curriculum that foregrounds computational foundations.
- Integration of authentic data science practices with foundational computer science skills using block-based programming and APIs.
- Empirical evidence from implementation in a diverse high school setting showing student engagement, skill development, and positive attitudes.
- Resources and pedagogical strategies supporting teachers and students in computational data science education.
