# 1021 · Camera, Action, False Alert! Tackling the Flood of False Alerts in Smart Home Cameras

## At a glance

- **Authors:** Sunyoung Kim, Xiaohuan Zhang, Ge Gao
- **Year:** 2025

## Paper overview

This study investigates how older adults use smart home cameras, focusing on the problem of frequent false motion detection alerts. Through interviews and analysis of Amazon reviews, the authors identify that false alerts and complex settings frustrate older users, reducing trust and usability. They propose design improvements to make these cameras more accessible and reliable, supporting older adults' independence and safety at home.

### Why it matters

**Research problem:** Smart home cameras generate frequent false motion detection alerts that overwhelm users, especially older adults, leading to frustration, alert fatigue, and reduced trust in the technology.

**Why it matters:** Older adults increasingly use smart home cameras to support aging in place and maintain safety and autonomy. False alerts undermine these benefits by causing users to ignore notifications, risking missed security events and reducing the technology's effectiveness.

**Key contributions:**

- Identification of false alerts as the primary challenge in smart home camera use by older adults.
- Detailed analysis of user struggles with motion sensitivity settings, motion zones, and notification management.
- Design recommendations to improve accessibility and usability of smart home cameras for older adults.
- Proposal to incorporate human-in-the-loop (HITL) feedback to improve motion detection accuracy and user control.
