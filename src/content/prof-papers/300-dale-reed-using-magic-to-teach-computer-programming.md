## At a glance

## Paper overview

This paper explores how magic tricks can be used as engaging, project-based teaching tools to motivate students and provide meaningful contexts for learning computer programming concepts such as variables, loops, decision statements, and binary numbers. It presents several magic-based programming assignments and demonstrations, including 'Choose a Number' and 'Pick a Card' tricks, that captivate student interest and encourage learning through curiosity and wonder.

### Why it matters

**Research problem:** How to effectively motivate and engage students in learning fundamental computer programming concepts by leveraging curiosity and interest through magic-based instructional methods.

**Why it matters:** Programming can be challenging and abstract for beginners; using magic tricks can stimulate curiosity, increase motivation, and promote higher-order thinking skills, leading to better learning outcomes and sustained interest in computer science.

**Key contributions:**

- Development of magic-based programming assignments that teach fundamental programming concepts.
- Demonstration of how magic tricks can illustrate computing concepts like binary numbers, parity, and search algorithms.
- Provision of online playable versions, assignment descriptions, and resources to facilitate adoption.
- Use of magic tricks in outreach presentations reaching thousands of students with positive feedback.
