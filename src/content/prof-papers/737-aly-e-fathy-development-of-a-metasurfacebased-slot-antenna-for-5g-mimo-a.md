# 737 · Development of a metasurface‑based slot antenna for 5G MIMO applications with minimized cross‑polarization and stable radiation patterns through mode manipulation

## Paper overview

This paper presents a novel design for a compact metasurface antenna optimized for 5G millimeter-wave MIMO systems. The antenna uses characteristic mode analysis to manipulate surface current modes, reducing unwanted higher-order modes and cross-polarization, while maintaining stable radiation patterns and high isolation between antenna elements. The design achieves a 41% size reduction compared to conventional designs and demonstrates excellent performance in both single and MIMO configurations.

### Why it matters

**Research problem:** Designing compact, high-performance metasurface antennas for 5G mm-wave MIMO systems is challenging due to the presence of unwanted higher-order modes that degrade gain, increase cross-polarization, and distort radiation patterns. Additionally, closely spaced antenna elements in MIMO configurations suffer from mutual coupling and beam squint, which impair system performance.

**Why it matters:** 5G mm-wave systems require antennas that support high data rates and low latency with compact size for practical deployment in devices and base stations. Minimizing cross-polarization and maintaining stable radiation patterns are critical for reliable communication and efficient MIMO operation. Addressing these challenges enables better system capacity, coverage, and user experience in emerging wireless technologies.

**Key contributions:**

- Novel metasurface antenna design with asymmetric patches incorporating slits and stubs to manipulate characteristic modes.
- Significant reduction (~41%) in antenna size compared to conventional metasurface patch antennas.
- Effective suppression of higher-order modes and elimination of mode degeneracy to reduce cross-polarization.
- Stable broadside radiation patterns across a wide bandwidth (23–31 GHz).
- Demonstration of high isolation (>18 dB and >21 dB) and low envelope correlation coefficient (<0.003) in compact MIMO configurations without additional spacing.
