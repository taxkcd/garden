## Paper overview

This study investigates how sharing outdoor experiences through digital diaries can help distant family members maintain emotional connections and have deeper conversations. Using a diary app called DailyBean, 22 participants recorded and shared their outdoor activities with family members over three weeks. The research identified key ways diary-sharing supports family bonds, such as starting new conversations, recalling memories, sharing interests, doing joint activities, and planning future events. The study offers design recommendations for communication tools that foster meaningful family interactions across distances.

### Why it matters

**Research problem:** Maintaining emotional connections and meaningful communication among distant family members is challenging. Existing technologies often enable quick updates but lack support for initiating engaging topics and sustaining deep emotional exchanges, especially for sharing rich outdoor experiences.

**Why it matters:** Distant families often experience surface-level conversations that lack emotional depth due to asynchronous schedules, time zone differences, and communication habit gaps. Outdoor experiences, which are emotionally rich, are difficult to share effectively with current digital tools, limiting opportunities for meaningful family communication and emotional closeness.

**Key contributions:**

- Demonstrated the value of digital diaries as a medium for maintaining emotional bonds and fostering deeper family relationships.
- Explored how diary content acts as conversational prompts facilitating intimate communication and emotional engagement.
- Examined how distant families integrate diary-sharing into communication routines, influenced by communication habits and trust.
- Provided design implications for computer-mediated communication technologies that prioritize relational depth and habitual meaningful exchanges.
