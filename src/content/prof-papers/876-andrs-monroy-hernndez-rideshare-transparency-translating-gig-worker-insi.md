## Paper overview

This paper investigates how rideshare platforms use AI and algorithms in ways that lack transparency, causing financial, emotional, and physical harm to drivers. Through interviews and analysis of over a million Reddit comments, the authors identify key transparency gaps in promotions, fares, routes, and task allocation. They propose policy solutions, including mandatory transparency reports by platforms, to improve drivers' working conditions and well-being.

### Why it matters

**Research problem:** Rideshare platforms exert significant control over drivers through opaque algorithmic management, resulting in harms due to information asymmetry and lack of transparency about promotions, fares, routes, and task allocation.

**Why it matters:** Opaque platform algorithms cause financial instability, psychological stress, and physical risks for gig workers. Drivers cannot make fully informed decisions, which exacerbates precarity and harms in gig work. Existing research lacks concrete design and policy solutions to address transparency issues.

**Key contributions:**

- Comprehensive identification of rideshare workers’ transparency concerns, mitigation strategies, and needs.
- Empirical evidence from mixed methods: in-depth interviews and large-scale LLM analysis of Reddit discussions.
- Proposal of a detailed roadmap for rideshare transparency reports as a policy solution to improve worker well-being.
- Demonstration of the effectiveness of LLMs for large-scale thematic analysis of unstructured online forum data.
- Grounding design recommendations in real-world driver interactions with platform AI and algorithmic features.
