# 540 · Web-Based, Interactive, Interest-Based Negotiation Training for Managing Conflict in Isolated Environments: Opportunistic Study With an e-Survey

## At a glance

- **Professor:** Kim Binsted

- **Year:** 2023

## Paper overview

This study developed and tested a web-based interactive training module that teaches interest-based negotiation (IBN) skills to help manage conflicts effectively, especially in isolated and confined environments like space missions or Antarctic stations. The module uses scenarios and decision points to engage users in learning negotiation techniques that prioritize relationships and mutual benefits. Participants found the training realistic, valuable, and enjoyable, suggesting it could be a useful tool for individuals in high-stakes, isolated settings.

### Why it matters

**Research problem:** Effective conflict management is critical in isolated and confined environments (ICEs) such as long-duration space missions, where poor negotiation can jeopardize mission success. Traditional positional bargaining is inadequate for these high-stakes, relationship-dependent situations, and there is a need for accessible, self-directed training in interest-based negotiation.

**Why it matters:** Interpersonal conflicts in ICEs have historically led to mission shortening or termination, as seen in multiple space missions. Since crew members may be isolated with those they need to negotiate with, training must be self-directed and accessible anytime. Improving negotiation skills can prevent mission failure and maintain team cohesion.

**Key contributions:**

- Development of a novel, self-directed, web-based interactive training module teaching interest-based negotiation tailored for ICEs.
- Incorporation of realistic scenarios and decision points to actively engage users in experiential learning.
- Evaluation of the module with participants in actual isolated and confined environments and during the COVID-19 pandemic.
- Demonstration that the module is perceived as realistic, valuable for learning, and enjoyable by diverse users.
- Provision of a flexible training tool that can be used for refresher training when conflicts arise.
