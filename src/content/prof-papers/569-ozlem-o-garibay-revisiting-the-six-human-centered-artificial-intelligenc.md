## Paper overview

This paper revisits six foundational challenges in human-centered artificial intelligence (HCAI) in light of the recent advances in generative AI (GenAI). It identifies new requirements and knowledge gaps introduced by GenAI, such as preserving human autonomy, ensuring safety despite non-deterministic outputs, and managing intellectual property complexities. The authors propose an updated research agenda focused on empowering humans and aligning AI development with human values to ensure AI systems enhance human dignity and collective well-being.

### Why it matters

**Research problem:** How to adapt and operationalize the six grand challenges of human-centered AI to address the novel disruptions and risks introduced by generative AI technologies, ensuring AI systems remain aligned with human values, autonomy, and societal well-being.

**Why it matters:** Generative AI is rapidly transforming many domains by creating new content and augmenting human capabilities, but its development is outpacing governance and oversight. Without addressing emerging risks such as loss of autonomy, bias, privacy violations, and accountability gaps, AI systems may cause harm, reduce human agency, and undermine trust.

**Key contributions:**

- Reaffirmation that the original six HCAI grand challenges remain essential organizing principles despite GenAI advances.
- Identification of new critical requirements such as preserving human autonomy, operational safety, and intellectual property navigation.
- Detailed analysis of GenAI impacts on human well-being, responsibility, privacy, security, governance, and cognitive alignment.
- Proposal of specific research questions and directions to address emerging challenges in GenAI.
- Emphasis on shifting from risk mitigation to human empowerment as the core principle for future AI development.
