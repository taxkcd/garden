- **Year:** 2026

## Paper overview

This paper presents Perspectra, an interactive multi-agent system that helps users engage with multiple AI agents representing different expert perspectives to improve critical thinking during research ideation. The system uses a forum-style interface with features like @-mentions, threaded discussions, and a mind map visualization to organize and structure agent deliberations. A user study with 18 participants showed that Perspectra significantly enhanced critical thinking behaviors, interdisciplinary replies, and proposal quality compared to a baseline group-chat interface.

### Why it matters

**Research problem:** How can users effectively control, steer, and critically evaluate collaboration among multiple domain-expert AI agents in multi-agent systems to improve interdisciplinary research ideation and critical thinking?

**Why it matters:** Interdisciplinary research requires reconciling diverse assumptions and methods, which benefits from critical thinking and dialogue among experts. Current multi-agent systems often lack fine-grained user control and structured interfaces, leading to cognitive overload and difficulty in sensemaking, which limits their effectiveness in supporting complex knowledge work.

**Key contributions:**

- Design of Perspectra system enabling user control to select expert personas and steer multi-agent deliberation with structured argumentation acts.
- Empirical evidence from a user study showing Perspectra significantly promotes higher-order critical thinking activities compared to a group-chat baseline.
- Demonstration that Perspectra users revised their research proposals more frequently and achieved greater quality improvements.
- Identification of both designed and emergent user behaviors, such as deep dives and verification checks, highlighting system flexibility.
- Design implications advocating adversarial agent responses and hybrid interaction models balancing user control and agent autonomy.
